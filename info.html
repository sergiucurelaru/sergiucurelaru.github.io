Location

Banffy Castle, Bonțida

The castle is in the village of Bonțida, about 30 km from Cluj-Napoca.

Shuttle: free buses leave from the center of Cluj every 30 minutes.

Train: get off at Bonțida station, then walk 10 minutes.

Car: there is a parking lot next to the village, but it fills up really fast.
