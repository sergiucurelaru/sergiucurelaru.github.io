Why Neon Castle?

A real castle

The concerts happen in the courtyard and gardens of a 17th century castle. Breathe in the ecstatic air!

Big names

Six international headliners on one stage, from punk rock to synth pop, from 14:00 until midnight.

Easy to get to

Free transportation from Cluj-Napoca and a train station in the village. No need to drive.
